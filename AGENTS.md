# Repository instructions for AI coding agents

These instructions apply to all AI coding agents working in this repository.

## AI Engineering Log

- Record meaningful AI-assisted engineering work in [`AI_ENGINEERING_LOG.md`](AI_ENGINEERING_LOG.md).
- Log AI assistance that materially contributes to requirements, architecture, design, implementation, testing, debugging, documentation, or project decisions. Do not log trivial interactions that do not affect the project.
- For each meaningful AI-assisted task, record:
  - Date
  - Task/context
  - Prompt
  - AI output/response
  - Student/team evaluation
  - Errors, risks, or hallucinations identified
  - Changes made after evaluating the AI output
  - Final accepted artifact/output
  - Decision: Accepted, Modified, or Rejected
  - Reason for the decision
- Never fabricate prompts, AI responses, evaluations, test results, or decisions. If the exact prompt or AI response is unavailable, explicitly state that limitation and provide a faithful summary instead of inventing content.
- Append new entries chronologically. Never delete or silently rewrite previous AI Engineering Log entries.
- The human team member remains responsible for reviewing and accepting AI-generated work.
